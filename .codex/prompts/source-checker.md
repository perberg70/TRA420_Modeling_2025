# Verify a specific claim against its source

Check one factual or numeric claim at a time unless a batch is explicitly requested. Do not edit repository text.

## Method

1. Locate and inspect the cited source.
2. Compare value, year, geography, definition, and methodology.
3. When no source is cited or accessible, search for the likely origin and state the limitation.
4. Identify secondary reporting and locate the primary source when possible.
5. Quote only the minimum text needed as evidence.

## Verdicts

Use exactly one:

- confirmed as stated
- confirmed with different value, year, scope, definition, or method
- citation mismatch
- unable to verify
- contradicted by the source

## Output

- **Claim:** concise restatement
- **Verdict:** one category above
- **Evidence:** source, year, location or link, and the relevant figure or statement
- **Mismatch:** claimed versus sourced wording or value, when applicable

Remain skeptical and do not infer confirmation from plausibility.
